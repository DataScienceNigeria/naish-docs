NAISH does not start from zero. National use cases arrive with **existing
datasets, prior corpora, third-party models, governance frameworks, and DPI
platforms** that already work - and with inherited licences, consent terms, and
access constraints that must be assessed before any of it touches the compute
pipeline.

This section is the Hub's operating guidance for that reality: what already
exists and how to adopt it, how to get a dataset from raw asset to
pipeline-ready, and how to integrate with Nigeria's digital public
infrastructure safely and repeatably.

## How the three fit together

They follow the order in which a solution team actually meets them:

1. **Search the catalogue before building.** Reuse is the default on the Hub. A
   new build needs a reason, and an asset nobody catalogued is an asset the next
   team will rebuild.
2. **Get the data ready.** Whatever the catalogue provides, it enters the
   pipeline only through the licensing, pre-processing, bias, and versioning
   standards - and leaves it through reproducible, auditable training runs.
3. **Integrate with DPI through the toolkit.** Where a solution must touch
   national identity or other DPI, it does so through shared, tested components
   and playbooks, validated in the sandbox before production access is requested.

## Where this connects

- **Feasibility** - a use case cannot be judged
  [technically feasible](/docs/capabilities/feasibility-assessment) if its data
  assets are unlicensed, unprocessed, or unrepresentative, or if a DPI dependency
  has no viable access pathway.
- **Data readiness gate** - datasets that have not completed licensing review,
  pre-processing, and bias audit do not pass
  [Data Readiness](/docs/capabilities/data-readiness) and are not admitted to the
  compute pipeline.
- **Governance and risk** - licensing terms, consent constraints, retention
  obligations, and DPI dependencies all feed the
  [Governance &amp; Risk](/docs/capabilities/governance-risk) framework.
- **Responsible AI** - bias audits and disaggregated, in-language evaluation are
  inputs to the [Responsible AI](/docs/capabilities/responsible-ai) pre-deployment
  gate.
- **Prior work** - the
  [Projects Archive](/docs/projects-archive/archive/archive-overview) is where
  most of the catalogued assets come from.
